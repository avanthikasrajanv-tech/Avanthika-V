About Avanthika V

Strategic digital marketing built upon analytical engineering principles, creative storytelling, and cutting-edge tools.

Passionate Digital Marketer & Creative Storyteller

Hello! I am Avanthika V (also known as Nayana Prakasan), a dedicated and results-focused digital marketer based in Kerala. I specialize in creative content creation, online branding, and developing comprehensive digital marketing strategies that turn casual scrollers into loyal customers.

My journey into marketing is powered by a distinctive multidisciplinary foundation. Holding a Diploma in Computer Engineering from GPTC Meppadi and having completed my Plus Two in Biology Science at GHSS Velliyode, I bring a structured, logical, and analytical perspective to every marketing campaign. Further, my experience working for one year as an Operator at Tata Electronics System Solution instilled rigorous attention to detail, process precision, and problem-solving agility.

To channel this analytical mindset into business growth, I completed an intensive six-month professional Digital Marketing course at Softronics. By combining technical knowledge with creative design instincts and modern AI-integrated digital marketing tools, I craft end-to-end campaigns that are as data-driven as they are emotionally engaging.

Recognized locally as an ambitious strategist striving to deliver the services of the Best Digital Marketer in Calicut, my vision is to help regional and global businesses establish an authoritative online presence through authentic brand voice, high-intent search visibility, and performance-driven campaigns.
